## Bio

- Joe helps run a vintage and curiosities shop in the outskirts of Wellington. He began his thrifting hobby while he was a student in high school trying to find goods to turn over for a profit, as well as to save money on clothes and other goods. Joe spends his mornings with his coffee browsing online marketplaces for anything he can resell or restore. He is not wealthy so when he is on the hunt he is resourceful, patient, and has a great eye for value.
